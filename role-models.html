👩‍💻 Women Who Changed The World Forever

From early computer programming and spaceflight to life-saving medicine and wireless Wi-Fi, meet the trailblazing women whose ideas shaped our modern world.

💻 Computing

Ada Lovelace

1815 – 1852

English mathematician who wrote the world's first algorithm intended to be carried out by Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine, foreseeing that computers could create music and art.

✨ First Computer Programmer
🚀 Space & Maths

Katherine Johnson

1918 – 2020

Brilliant NASA mathematician whose orbital mechanics calculations were critical to the success of the first US crewed spaceflights and the Apollo 11 Moon landing.

✨ NASA Orbital Trajectory Pioneer
💻 Computing

Grace Hopper

1906 – 1992

Computer scientist and US Navy Rear Admiral who invented one of the first compilers and helped create COBOL, proving code could be written using English-like words.

✨ Pioneer of Modern Programming Languages
🔬 Physics & Chemistry

Marie Curie

1867 – 1934

Physicist and chemist who pioneered research on radioactivity, discovered polonium and radium, and became the first person to win two Nobel Prizes in two different sciences.

✨ Two-Time Nobel Laureate
💻 Wireless Tech

Hedy Lamarr

1914 – 2000

Actress and self-taught inventor who co-created a frequency-hopping communication system that laid the foundation for modern Wi-Fi, GPS, and Bluetooth technologies.

✨ Co-Inventor of Frequency-Hopping Tech
🩺 Genetics & Chemistry

Rosalind Franklin

1920 – 1958

British chemist and X-ray crystallographer whose famous "Photo 51" was essential to discovering the double-helix structure of human DNA.

✨ Key to Unlocking the Structure of DNA
🚀 Space & Medicine

Dr. Mae Jemison

Born 1956

Engineer, physician, and former NASA astronaut who became the first African American woman to travel into space aboard the Space Shuttle Endeavour in 1992.

✨ First Black Woman in Space
💻 Software Engineering

Margaret Hamilton

Born 1936

Computer scientist who led the team that built the onboard flight software for NASA's Apollo missions and helped coin the term "software engineering."

✨ Led Apollo Flight Software Team
🩺 Medicine

Tu Youyou

Born 1930

Pharmaceutical chemist who discovered artemisinin, a breakthrough malaria treatment that has saved millions of lives worldwide, earning her the 2015 Nobel Prize in Medicine.

✨ Discovered Life-Saving Malaria Treatment
🔬 Nuclear Physics

Chien-Shiung Wu

1912 – 1997

Experimental physicist known as the "First Lady of Physics," whose famous Wu Experiment overturned a major law of nuclear physics and transformed particle science.

✨ Groundbreaking Experimental Physicist
🚀 Satellite Maths

Dr. Gladys West

Born 1930

Mathematician whose precise satellite geodesy models of Earth's shape became the mathematical backbone of modern Global Positioning System (GPS) navigation.

✨ Mathematician Behind GPS
💻 Networking

Radia Perlman

Born 1951

Computer programmer and network engineer who invented the Spanning Tree Protocol (STP), a fundamental algorithm that allows massive internet networks to run smoothly.

✨ Pioneer of Internet Network Architecture
